Where to find the numbers
  • Median sold: realestate.com.au or Domain suburb profiles, Cotality (CoreLogic) reports, NSW Valuer General sales data.
  • Average sold: average the "Sold" results for the month on realestate.com.au / Domain, or use NSW Valuer General sales data.
  • Average listing: average the asking prices of current "Buy" listings, or SQM Research asking-price data.
